- Status: normalized from a generic Springer guide. The Festschrift editors' invitation and volume-specific instructions take precedence.

## Manuscript Elements

- Prepare the chapter in Springer's proceedings LaTeX or Word template requested by the editors.
- Include final title, author names, affiliations, and corresponding-author information.
- ORCID identifiers are encouraged.
- Include a self-contained abstract and keywords.
- Include acknowledgments, disclosure of interests, and other declarations required by the volume.
- Use a structured heading hierarchy; the guide recommends numbering only the first two heading levels.

## References

- Numeric citations are conventional in LNCS and are the working choice for this article.
- Include DOI links where available.
- Ensure every reference cited in the text appears in the bibliography and vice versa.
- Avoid informal or unverifiable sources where a primary scholarly publication exists.

## Tables and Figures

- Keep tables editable rather than embedding them as raster images.
- Put table captions above tables and figure captions below figures.
- Provide figures at publication quality.
- Current official guidance requires alt text or equivalent accessibility descriptions for non-text content.
- Reusing a thesis figure may require permission. A newly drawn conceptual table or figure is preferable if it adds value.

## Submission Package

- Submit editable source files and a PDF that exactly matches those sources.
- Include all figure assets, bibliography files, and required declarations.
- Remove comments, tracked changes, and unresolved placeholders before submission.

## Length and Language

- The generic guide describes full papers as commonly at least 12 to 15 pages and short papers as approximately 6 to 11 pages. This is not a confirmed limit for the Festschrift.
- Use American English unless the volume editors specify otherwise.
