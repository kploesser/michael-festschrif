# Michael Rosemann Festschrift Article Brief

## Status

The article scope is confirmed and a first manuscript exists. Volume-specific submission constraints and several personal author inputs remain open.

## Commission

Prepare Karsten Ploesser's contribution to an LNCS Festschrift honoring Professor Michael Rosemann's contributions to Business Process Management.

The chapter should show the evolution from Karsten's 2013 research on context-aware information systems to present advances in artificial intelligence, including agentic systems that interact with and adjust their actions to their environment.

## Proposed Title

*Context Is More Than a Window: From Context-Aware Information Systems to Agentic Business Processes*

## Central Question

What can the design theory for context-aware information systems contribute to the design and governance of contemporary agentic business processes?

## Central Claim

Agentic AI does not eliminate the original context-awareness problem. It turns that problem into an operational and governance problem at run time. An LLM context window can hold instructions, retrieved documents, memories, observations, and tool results, but it does not determine which environmental information is relevant, how a socio-technical work system should respond, when an action should be triggered, or how learning should be governed.

## Proposed Contribution

The chapter reconstructs the five CAIS meta-requirements from the 2013 thesis and maps them to five proposed design principles for context-aware agentic process systems:

| 2013 CAIS meta-requirement | Proposed agentic-process principle |
| --- | --- |
| Represent the work system | Frame the socio-technical system, goals, roles, and constraints |
| Represent relevant context | Select, maintain, and qualify environmental observations |
| Specify adaptation strategies | Provide sufficient but bounded action variety |
| Match situations to strategies | Select actions with traceability, timing, and escalation |
| Learn by modifying representations and strategies | Govern memory, reflection, policy change, and self-modification |

The mapping is a conceptual synthesis and research agenda. It should not be presented as an empirically validated extension of the 2013 design theory.

## Intellectual Lineage

The chapter's Festschrift logic is circular rather than ceremonial:

1. Rosemann, Recker, and Flender's early context-aware BPM work moved attention from intrinsic process flexibility to environmental drivers.
2. Karsten's joint work with Michael and Jan Recker developed the empirical and design-science basis for context-aware process design.
3. Karsten's thesis generalized this work into a design theory for context-aware information systems.
4. Contemporary language agents operationalize perception, reasoning, action, memory, and feedback in interactive environments.
5. AI-augmented and agentic BPM now confront governance questions that the earlier context-aware systems work anticipated.
6. Rosemann and coauthors' recent drifts from transactions to conversations and automation to autonomization provide a direct contemporary return to the original theme.

## Festschrift Tone

The chapter should be academically useful even outside the commemorative volume. The tribute appears through:

- the choice of a question rooted in Michael's work;
- a brief first-person account of the doctoral research and supervision;
- an argument that follows the idea into a new technological era;
- a conclusion that credits Michael's habit of widening the frame of BPM.

One specific personal recollection from Karsten would add authenticity. Generic praise should not substitute for that detail.

## Audience

- BPM and information-systems researchers.
- Researchers in AI-augmented and agentic BPM.
- Practitioners interested in adaptive and governed process systems.
- Festschrift readers familiar with Michael's work but not necessarily with Karsten's thesis.

## Evidence Boundaries

- The thesis is the authority for the 2013 design theory.
- Primary publications support contemporary technical claims.
- Public demonstrations and benchmarks establish possibilities and limitations, not production reliability.
- No post-2013 personal research trajectory should be inferred without Karsten's confirmation.
- No proprietary or confidential industry example should be introduced without explicit clearance.
