# Michael Rosemann Festschrift Article

This repository develops Karsten Ploesser's contribution to an LNCS Festschrift honoring Professor Michael Rosemann's contributions to Business Process Management.

## Article Prompt

Draft a scholarly and reflective article showing the evolution from Karsten's 2013 research on context-aware information systems to contemporary AI, including agentic systems that can interact with and adjust their actions to their environment.

## Working Thesis

Agentic AI makes the original context-awareness design problem more urgent rather than obsolete. A large model context window does not by itself create context-awareness. Effective agentic process systems must still represent the socio-technical system, select relevant environmental context, maintain a bounded repertoire of adaptation strategies, connect situations to actions, and learn from outcomes under explicit governance.
